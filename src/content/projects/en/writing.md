## Problem

Emirhan researches and writes on science (especially physics), philosophy (religion, science, ontology) and history.

## Approach

He publishes his essays as "ufens" on Evrim Ağacı, on his own Blogger and WordPress blogs, and on Academia; the same essay often appears in more than one place.

## Result

Six essays between November 2022 and 2025. All of them are in the [Writing](/en/blog/) section of this site, in Turkish and English; for “Why Was İskilipli Atıf Hanged?” there is the abstract and a link to the full paper on Academia.
