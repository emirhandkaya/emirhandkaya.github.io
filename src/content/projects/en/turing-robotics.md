## Problem

The FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is an international high-school robotics competition in which teams design, build and program a robot for each season's game on a tight schedule.

## Approach

I founded Turing Robotics at Halil Bekmezci Anadolu Lisesi in 2025 and served as team captain; today I continue as lead mentor. The team is organised into finance, communications and engineering groups. Robotics built my engineering, leadership and problem-solving skills.

## Result

- **2026 İstanbul Regional champions** ("Regional Winners", season game _REBUILT presented by Haas_), in the team's rookie season.
- Qualified for the **FIRST Championship** in Houston.
- I told the team's story to GirişimcilikTürk TV on 9 April 2026: ["From Türkiye to the World Stage: A Robotics Team's Success Story"](https://girisimciturk.tv/girisimcilik/turkiyeden-dunya-sahnesine-bir-robotik-takiminin-basari-hikayesi/) (in Turkish).

## 2027 season

- **Events:** the 2027 Bosphorus Regional and the 2027 Gotham Regional (New York).
- **Supporters:** grants from **Boeing**, **3M**, **RTX** and **Rockwell** for the 2027 season.
