## Problem

The FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is an international high-school robotics competition in which teams design, build and program a robot for each season's game.

## Approach

Turing Robotics is the FRC team of Halil Bekmezci Anadolu Lisesi (team number 10141, rookie year 2025). The team is organised into finance, communications and engineering groups. Emirhan is a member of the team; a GirişimcilikTürk TV article describes him as one of its prominent members.

## Result

- **"Regional Winners"** at the 2026 İstanbul Regional (season game: _REBUILT presented by Haas_).
- The team qualified for the FIRST Championship in Houston.
- The team's story was covered by GirişimcilikTürk TV on 9 April 2026: "From Türkiye to the World Stage: A Robotics Team's Success Story".
