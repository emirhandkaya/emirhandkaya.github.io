One of the issues still debated in present-day Turkey is why İskilipli Atıf Hoca was hanged. Although some circles claim that he was executed solely because of his opposition to the hat, this view does not fully reflect the truth. The real reason was that İskilipli Atıf engaged in opposing activities aimed at eliminating the Republican regime and the National Struggle. For this reason, his hanging is not merely a matter of a hat. To understand this important issue, it will be useful to briefly address who the leading figures opposed to the National Struggle in that period were, beginning with İskilipli Atıf, and what kinds of activities they engaged in.
