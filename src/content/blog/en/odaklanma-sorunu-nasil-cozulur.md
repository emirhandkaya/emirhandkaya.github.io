<!-- image: How Can the Focus Problem Be Solved? -->

I say "problem" with focus because being unable to focus really is a problem in the full sense of the word. When a person cannot focus, they do their work wrongly, and it can even lead to disappointment through the feeling of "I can't do it".

Is focusing something within a person's control?

In general, yes: focusing or not being able to focus is something within a person's control. I said in general because in some cases it is a problem that is not within a person's control; for example, in people with ADHD (attention deficit hyperactivity disorder) this problem is not in their hands. Because ADHD is a psychiatric condition, though in a way the solution to this condition is also in a person's hands. ADHD can generally be treated with therapy or medication. As I said at the start, apart from such cases, focusing is a problem that is within a person's control.

## So what are the solutions to this problem?

There is no clear-cut solution, but there are some ways that can solve this problem if they are applied fully. However, as I said, the solution to this problem is in the person's hands. If a person does not do certain things consistently and willingly for the solution, then it is not a struggle toward a solution but merely a period of wasted time. So doing the methods I am about to mention consistently and willingly is a sure path to the solution.

You know the importance of sleep, more or less. I can explain its importance to you like this: go back in time and remember how you felt on the mornings you woke up toward noon or without enough sleep; you probably woke up feeling bad, weak, sleepy and unwilling. That is why you need to get your sleep properly. Scientifically, the hours of sleep by age are as follows:

And especially in the growth stage called adolescence, getting good sleep is important for growth and development. In short, getting good sleep is one of the most necessary elements for focus and productive days.

## NUTRITION

Like sleep, if nutrition is lacking, it clearly leads not only to focus problems but also to health problems.

The main element to pay attention to in nutrition consists of what we call macronutrients: "proteins, fats, carbohydrates, fiber, macrominerals and water". Among these, apart from water, the three basic nutrients are protein, fat and carbohydrate. And these three basic nutrients should be consumed regularly and in sufficient amounts. The table below shows what kinds of foods you can use to get these macronutrients in sufficient amounts.

<!-- image: table of foods rich in each macronutrient -->

However, there are some points in this table that you should pay attention to. For example, in the carbohydrate section, when taking carbohydrates it will be better to get them from quality carbohydrate sources such as rice, oats and the like, instead of low-quality carbohydrate sources such as sugar. You need to take fats and carbohydrates in the amount you need, without overdoing it. If you are not an athlete, taking excessive amounts of fat and carbohydrate can lead to fat accumulation, that is, weight problems. And these macronutrients have corresponding calorie values.

For example, on average:

1 g protein: 4 calories

1 g carbohydrate: 4 calories

1 g fat: 9 calories

That is why calculating calories will be more sensible and orderly.

## SOCIAL MEDIA

Social media, in fact, does not cause any focus problem as long as it is not overused. However, as you will see from the current statistics and from looking around you, we spend more time on social media than necessary, or in other words, we kill time. You can see the average social media statistics of people around the world in the table below.

<!-- image: table of average social media usage statistics -->

As you can see in the table, the average time a person spends on social media per day comes to about 2.30 hours. And let us underline that this is only "social media". We can attribute the figure of 2.30 hours to older people spending little and young people spending a lot. A study carried out on young people, who are the ones who mostly experience focus problems, says the following about these statistics:

"In Turkey, the time young people spend on social media is 0-3 hours a day for 42% of them, 4-7 hours for 35%, 8-12 hours for 15%, and more than 12 hours for 8%. 58% of young people, that is, more than half, spend more than 3 hours a day on social media."

Speaking specifically about focus, social media used for these durations has become an attachment, and for those who spend long durations, an addiction. This attachment or addiction is a big problem for your focus. I will not go into the topic of social media being unnecessary here. But if I touch on it briefly: on social media everyone tries to look like the person they want to appear to be, what is on social media is not what is in the real world, and social media is wasted time. My suggestion is not necessarily that you get rid of it. My suggestion is that you do not trust social media and that you do not use it beyond certain durations.

Also remember: "people do not appear on social media as they are, but as they want to appear."

## Being Planned and Organized

A planned and organized work routine is quite important for focus. When people organize their work and do it in a planned and orderly way, it increases their focus and makes completing the work more efficient.

A planned and organized work routine helps people decide in advance which tasks they will do and when, and carry out tasks in order of priority. This enables people to manage their work more effectively and shorten the time it takes to complete it.

In addition, an organized work routine helps people organize their work better. Deciding in advance when tasks will be done creates a routine in people's brains and helps them do their work faster and more effectively. This improves focus skills and makes it easier to complete tasks.

A planned and organized work routine also helps people work more productively by increasing their motivation. Having a specific plan for completing tasks makes people more willing to reach their goals. This, by improving focus skills, helps tasks get completed faster and more effectively.

## PURPOSE AND DESIRE

People having certain purposes and goals, being willing, and making an effort to achieve them improves their focus skills and helps work get completed more productively.

Purposes and goals improve people's focus skills, because working toward a specific goal helps people concentrate their attention on that goal. For example, if a person has the goal of completing a particular task, they know they must focus and finish their work to reach this goal. This helps them gather the attention needed to complete the task and avoid unnecessary distractions.

Being willing is also one of the quite important elements for focus. People being willing toward the work they will do helps them do their work better. Being willing helps people do their work with more focus and complete tasks more effectively.

Purpose and desire also improve focus skills by increasing people's motivation. When people work toward a particular purpose or goal, they become more willing and motivated toward their work. This, by increasing their focus, helps them complete their work faster and more productively.
