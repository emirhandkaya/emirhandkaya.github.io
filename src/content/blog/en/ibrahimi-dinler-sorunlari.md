Welcome. First of all, I would like to state that the ideas, views and thoughts in this article are written from my own perspective. They are my own thoughts, which emerged from what I have researched and seen.

## The Singularity of the Concept of Religion

In this section I will address the singularity of the religions classified as the "Abrahamic religions", formed by Christianity, Judaism and Islam, which accept Abraham as their "forefather".

According to Islam, these religions and their books came into the world, at the very beginning, as books made by God. However, the Gospel, the Torah and the Psalms were later altered. The religions, or the concepts of religion, proclaimed by the Quran, the Gospel, the Torah and the Psalms existed before these religions as well; events such as the good going to one place and the wicked to another after death, or the existence of people with a mission called prophets, and many other things, appear in a similar way in religions that predate these. According to Islam, apart from these books, the true creator (God) has not made any book, and therefore any religion. Since religions are things made or invented by people, such Abrahamic religions have no privilege or sanctity over other religions with regard to the concept of religion. (For a theist,) a creator is not needed in order to make religions.

## The Sanctity of the Holy Books

In this section I will touch on a few points about the sanctity of books such as the Quran, the Gospel, the Torah and the Psalms.

The Abrahamic religions generally cite their own holy books as sources to prove their own truth; however, these books are, today, books made of ink and pages obtained from nature, and at the time they emerged too they were books that could be written, made and composed by people. In the periods of those civilizations and afterwards, very well-constructed stories and literary works were written. There is no feature or aspect that sets these books apart from other books and makes them holy, or that prevents them from being fabricated or made by people.

## The Reality of the Prophets and Their Miracles

In this section I will address the miracles the prophets are claimed to have performed, and the reliability and reality of the hadiths Muhammad is claimed to have spoken.

The prophets we are talking about in the Abrahamic religions lived in very, very ancient times, centuries ago, and we know of their existence only from accounts, information and writings that have reached us and whose truth is uncertain. Besides their existence, there are also miracles they are claimed to have performed; however, the truth and reality of these deeds rest on nothing concrete, only on hearsay. People can also claim that others have done extraordinary things. Now let us come to the hadiths: almost all of them are based on narrations. In books where hadiths are collected, such as Riyad as-Salihin, almost every hadith is preceded by notes such as "narrated from Umayra" or "as narrated from Aisha". That is, the hadiths rest on narrations.

## Closing Words

Of course, what I have said here is not a list of faults in these concepts but my own interpretation. Believing in them is a choice, and so is not believing. What I have written does not show that these concepts are false; it only emphasizes that they are things people could also have produced.
