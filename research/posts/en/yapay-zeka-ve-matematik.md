Artificial intelligence is undoubtedly one of the best inventions of our time, and according to some, the best of all. Before explaining artificial intelligence, and then the relationship between AI and mathematics, let us look at the importance of artificial intelligence.

#### The Importance of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is undoubtedly one of the most important inventions of our age, and indeed of our day. To understand the importance of AI better, we can compare it to literacy. Centuries ago, literacy was very important. By literacy I mean the ability to read and write books, articles, compositions, letters and so on. To give an example, back then, if you wanted to talk to someone far away, you either had to be able to write and read letters or to know someone who could. Later, around the middle of the 1900s, with the arrival in our lives of digital inventions such as the computer and then the mobile phone, the concept of digital literacy also entered our lives. And when it arrived, literacy was an advantage just as it had been centuries earlier. Of course, this concept changed a little and became digital literacy. What we call digital literacy was a great privilege in the late 90s and early 2000s; people were chosen for jobs accordingly, and could do certain things in their lives more easily and in less time. In my opinion, digital literacy has now given up its throne to AI literacy.

Artificial intelligence and technology are of great importance in almost every area of our lives. Thanks to AI, business processes can be carried out faster and more efficiently. With the development of technology, communication has become easier, and access to information faster and easier.

### What Is Artificial Intelligence?

I can explain artificial intelligence with an analogy: imagine something like a robot or a computer program that can think and make decisions. This robot or program learns on its own and improves itself using its experiences. Like humans, it can perceive, learn, and even, in a way, "think" and solve problems. Artificial intelligence helps people solve complex problems and can do some jobs quickly and efficiently without people needing to do them. In other words, artificial intelligence is a kind of technology that enables computers to think and do work on their own. Since AI does not get tired like a human and does not have needs that must be met, and since it is a system that works without stopping, this creates a great advantage.

#### Examples of Artificial Intelligence

Having explained AI at such length, it would not do to leave out examples. With examples we understand its areas of use better.

Here are some examples of artificial intelligence:

Voice Assistants: Voice assistants such as Siri, Alexa and Google Assistant interact with people using artificial intelligence technology and offer users different services.

Autonomous Driving: Artificial intelligence technology plays a major role in preventing traffic accidents and making travel safer by enabling vehicles to drive themselves.

Image Processing: Artificial intelligence technology is used in image processing in many areas such as recognizing objects and faces, classifying visual content, image restoration and image manipulation.

Machine Translation: Artificial intelligence technology makes it possible to translate texts between different languages quickly and accurately.

Game Bots: Artificial intelligence technology is used in developing game bots and enables bots to compete against human players.

Financial Forecasting: Artificial intelligence technology is used in financial forecasting, to predict financial indicators such as stock prices, exchange rates and cryptocurrencies.

Robotics: Artificial intelligence technology allows robots to interact with people and carry out various tasks. This field has many different areas of application such as industrial robots, healthcare robots, agricultural robots and service robots.

#### Where Did the Idea of Artificial Intelligence Come From?

Until today, technological inventions have emerged from attempts to make computers and systems do what physical, chemical and biological natural phenomena do. To give examples: eye-camera, bird-airplane, brain-artificial intelligence (artificial brain), whale-sonar and so on.

When humans came up with such inventions, they were not inspired only by natural phenomena. They also made inventions aimed at their needs. Artificial intelligence, our subject, likewise emerged from the attempt to transfer some functions of the brain to technology.

#### The Scope and Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence aims to create systems that can work and learn by thinking in ways similar to humans. These systems are built by bringing together many disciplines and draw on many fields such as mathematics, computer science, physics, psychology, biology and philosophy.

Mathematics is a discipline that forms the foundation of AI algorithms. AI algorithms work using mathematical concepts such as probability theory, matrix calculus, optimization, statistics and linear algebra. These concepts are the basic building blocks required for AI systems to operate.

Computer science has an important role in building AI systems. AI algorithms are used together with computer science concepts such as programming languages, data structures, algorithms and computer architecture. These concepts are necessary for programming AI systems and processing data.

Physics is used in many areas of AI systems. For example, robotic systems and autonomous vehicles involve mechanical and electrical concepts. AI systems can be built using optics, mechanics, electricity, magnetism, thermodynamics and other physical concepts.

Psychology is an important discipline for AI systems. AI draws on concepts related to the human mind. For example, emotion recognition, learning and behavioral psychological concepts form the basis of AI algorithms.

Biology is an important discipline in building AI systems. AI draws on biological concepts to imitate biological systems and the properties of biological organisms. For example, neural networks, genetic algorithms and other biological systems are used in AI algorithms.

Control theory can be thought of as a conductor directing an orchestra. The conductor steers the music as desired by balancing the sounds coming from the musicians' different instruments and adjusting tempo and emphasis. In the same way, control theory reaches goals by making the decisions necessary to steer a system or process and by balancing various inputs and outputs. Artificial intelligence, in this process, can use control theory techniques to bring systems, processes or devices to the desired results.

Philosophy is also an important discipline in the field of AI. AI technologies relate to consciousness, free will, ethics and other philosophical concepts. For example, ethical AI seeks answers to many philosophical questions about AI. Subjects such as the effects of AI on people, how autonomous AI systems should be, and how human-like AI should be fall within the field of ethical philosophy. Besides this, the concepts of consciousness and free will are also related to AI technologies. How "conscious" AI systems could be, and whether they could have free will, are matters of debate. For this reason, the role of philosophy in the field of AI is quite important.

### The Relationship Between Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics

The relationship between artificial intelligence and mathematics, two closely connected fields, is quite important. Mathematics provides the theoretical and computational principles that form the foundation of AI, and is used in the development, training and application of AI. Here are some topics used mathematically in AI and the products that support them:

1. Linear algebra: Linear algebra is a mathematical subject used in AI. Operations on matrices and vectors are widely used in areas such as image processing, natural language processing and data analytics. For example, Python-based libraries such as NumPy and TensorFlow are used in AI by making linear algebra operations easier.
2. Probability theory: Probability theory is used in AI algorithms to deal with uncertainty. For example, the prediction and classification algorithms used in AI work on the basis of probability theory. Statistical analysis tools such as R and MATLAB provide support for probability theory.
3. Optimization methods: Optimization methods are used for training and optimizing AI models. Gradient-based optimization algorithms are mathematical techniques frequently used in AI. Popular deep learning libraries such as TensorFlow, PyTorch and Keras work integrated with optimization methods.
4. Graph theory: Graph theory models complex structures in AI such as networks, relationships and data structures. For example, graph theory is used in social network analytics, transportation network optimization and genetic algorithms. Graph-theory-based libraries such as NetworkX and Gephi make graph-based analysis easier in AI.
5. Numerical analysis: Numerical analysis is a subject that forms the mathematical basis of AI. Mathematical concepts such as linear regression, logistic regression, derivatives and integrals form the basis of the models used in AI. Numerical analysis tools such as MATLAB, SciPy and NumPy support mathematical computation in AI.
6. Theoretical computation: Theoretical computation covers the computational theories at the foundation of AI. For example, theoretical computation concepts such as artificial neural networks, complexity theory, data structures and algorithms are used in developing AI models. As examples of languages used in theoretical computation, programming languages such as Python, C++ and Java support mathematical computation in AI.
7. Statistics: Statistics is used in AI in areas such as data analytics, prediction and decision-making. Evaluating the accuracy and reliability of the models used in AI relies on data analysis. Statistical analysis tools such as R, Python and SPSS support the application of statistical methods in AI.
8. Natural language processing: Natural language processing is used in AI for analyzing and making sense of language data. Language models are used in natural language processing applications such as text classification, sentiment analysis and text generation. Natural language processing libraries such as NLTK, spaCy and TensorFlow make processing language data easier in AI.
9. Data analytics: AI models work on the basis of data analytics. Data analytics involves exploring, cleaning, transforming and analyzing the data used in AI. Data analytics tools and platforms support data analytics processes in AI. For example, data analytics tools such as Tableau, Power BI and Google Analytics support data analysis in AI.

The mathematical topics and supporting products mentioned above provide the mathematical foundation for developing and applying AI. Mathematics enables AI to obtain accurate and reliable results and supports the successful use of AI in various areas of application.
